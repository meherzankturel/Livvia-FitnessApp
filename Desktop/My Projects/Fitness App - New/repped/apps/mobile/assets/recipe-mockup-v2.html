Ingredients 6 items
Black beans 1/2 can
White rice 1 cup
Corn 1/4 cup
Avocado 1/2
Salsa 3 tbsp
Sour cream 1 tbsp
Steps 4 steps
01
Cook rice according to package directions. Fluff with a fork when done.
~2 min
02
Heat black beans in a small pot over medium heat. Season with cumin and salt to taste.
~3 min
03
Slice avocado and prep remaining toppings — corn, salsa, sour cream.
~2 min
04
Assemble the bowl: rice base, black beans, corn, avocado slices, salsa, and a dollop of sour cream.
~1 min
