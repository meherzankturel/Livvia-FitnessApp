Breakfast Recipe
Veggie Stir Fry
with Tofu
Prep
10 min
Cook
12 min
Watch Recipe Video
Ingredients8 items
Firm tofu½ block cubed
Broccoli1 cup florets
Bell pepper1 sliced
Snap peas½ cup
Teriyaki sauce2 tbsp
White rice1 cup cooked
Sesame oil1 tbsp
Garlic2 cloves
Steps
1
Press tofu and cut into cubes. Pan-fry in sesame oil until golden on all sides.
2
Remove tofu. Add broccoli, pepper, snap peas and garlic. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes.
3
Return tofu to pan. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything. Toss to coat evenly.
4
Serve over fluffy white rice. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
