Breakfast Recipe
Veggie Stir Fry
with Tofu
10min prep
12min cook
Easy
Ingredients 8 items
Firm tofu½ block
Broccoli1 cup
Bell pepper1 sliced
Snap peas½ cup
Teriyaki sauce2 tbsp
White rice1 cup
Sesame oil1 tbsp
Garlic2 cloves
Steps 4 steps
01
Step 1 of 4
Press tofu and cut into cubes. Pan-fry in sesame oil until golden on all sides.
~4 min · Base camp
02
Step 2 of 4
Remove tofu. Add broccoli, pepper, snap peas and garlic. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes.
~3 min
03
Step 3 of 4
Return tofu to pan. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and toss to coat.
~2 min
04
Step 4 of 4
Serve over fluffy white rice. Garnish with sesame seeds.
~1 min · Summit
