Veggie Stir Fry with Tofu
10 min prep · 12 min cook · Easy
Ingredients8 items
Firm tofu½ block
Sesame oil1 tbsp
Broccoli1 cup
Bell pepper1 sliced
Snap peas½ cup
Teriyaki2 tbsp
White rice1 cup
Garlic2 cloves
Steps
1
Press tofu and cut into cubes. Pan-fry in sesame oil until golden on all sides, about 4 minutes.
2
Remove tofu. Add broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas and garlic. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
3
Return tofu to pan. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and toss to coat evenly.
4
Serve over fluffy white rice. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
