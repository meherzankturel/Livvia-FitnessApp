Veggie
Stir Fry
with Tofu
10
min prep
12
min cook
Ingredients
Firm tofu½ block cubed
Broccoli1 cup florets
Bell pepper1 sliced
Snap peas½ cup
Teriyaki sauce2 tbsp
White rice1 cup cooked
Sesame oil1 tbsp
Garlic2 cloves
Steps
01~4 min
Press tofu and cut into cubes. Pan-fry in sesame oil until golden on all sides.
02~3 min
Remove tofu. Add broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas and garlic. Stir-fry for three to four minutes until crisp-tender.
03~2 min
Return tofu to the pan. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and toss gently to coat all pieces evenly.
04~1 min
Serve immediately over fluffy white rice. Garnish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
