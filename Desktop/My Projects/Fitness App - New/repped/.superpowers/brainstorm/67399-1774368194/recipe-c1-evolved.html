Veggie Stir Fry
with Tofu

Ingredients

1 cup

½ cup

2 tbsp
Teriyaki sauce
1 cup
White rice, cooked
1 tbsp
Sesame oil
2 cloves
Garlic, minced
Steps
1
Press tofu and cut into cubes. Pan-fry in sesame oil over medium-high heat until golden on all sides, about 4 minutes.
2
Remove tofu and set aside. Add broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas and minced garlic to the hot pan. Stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
3
Return the tofu to the pan. Pour teriyaki sauce over everything and toss gently to coat all pieces evenly.
4
Serve immediately over fluffy white rice. Garnish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds if desired.
