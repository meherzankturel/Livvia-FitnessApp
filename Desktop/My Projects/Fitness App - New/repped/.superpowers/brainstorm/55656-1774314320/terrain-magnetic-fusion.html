The Fusion Concept
⛰
Every workout is an expedition. Terrain's earthy palette (#2D2A24 earth, #F6F5F0 sand, #34D399 trail green) creates a grounded, outdoorsy warmth. Magnetic's personal messaging and bento widgets bring the emotional hook. Together: an expedition journal that knows you.
From Magnetic
💬
Personal hero message — data-driven, changes daily. Adapted to terrain language: "Last push day you summited at 75kg. Today's route goes to 77.5kg."
🔥
Streak widget — tall, dominant, giant number. Added: mini elevation bars showing effort intensity per day (replacing flat bars).
📈
Bento layout — asymmetric grid. Momentum + volume widgets with contour line decoration inside.
From Terrain
🏔
Elevation profile — your week as a mountain range with a trail that draws itself. Added: a hiker dot with a pinging radius that travels along the trail, showing where you are right now.
📍
Waypoint exercises — connected by a living trail line with a pulse traveling downward. Each waypoint has an altitude number showing cumulative effort elevation.
🌎
Topo contour background — subtle contour lines drift behind the entire UI at 2.5% opacity. Also woven into individual cards as decoration.
Original Additions
🏁
Summit Target card. Shows today's peak challenge (your hardest lift goal) as a mountain summit. A progress bar fills as you approach it. "77.5kg Bench — 2.5kg above your last peak." Gives you a concrete thing to chase.
⛅
Trail Conditions — readiness reframed as weather. "Clear conditions" = fresh. "Foggy" = mild soreness. "Storm warning" = high fatigue. Fits the expedition metaphor perfectly.
🚶
Hiker dot — an animated dot with a pinging ring that travels along the elevation profile trail. Shows "you are here" in your week. No other fitness app has this.
📏
Altitude markers on each waypoint — cumulative "elevation" based on exercise volume. Base camp → 2,800 → 4,200 → summit. Gamifies the workout as vertical gain.
💓
Heartbeat system preserved from Living Organism — the trail-dot, streak glow, hiker ping, and CTA aura all pulse on a shared 2.4s rhythm. The expedition journal is alive.
Color System
🎨
Earth: #2D2A24 — primary text, cards, CTA
Sand: #F6F5F0 — background, light surfaces
Trail: #34D399 — active states, progress, hiker dot
Rock: #8E8E7A — secondary text, labels
Stone: #EDEBE5 — widget backgrounds
Accent: #6366F1 — streak bars, highlights (sparingly)
Expo Go Compatible
✅
Every effect uses Animated API only. Elevation SVG → replaced with View-based layered approach in RN. Gradients → layered opacity Views. Contour lines → absolute positioned rounded Views. Trail pulse → Animated.Value translateY loop. Hiker dot → Animated.ValueXY with spring.
