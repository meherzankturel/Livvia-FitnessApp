Why You Open This App
💬
The daily message changes every session. It's not "Good morning." It's a personal insight that only YOUR data can generate. It references your history, your streaks, your upcoming milestones. It gives you a reason to care today.
Examples of daily messages: "You're 1 session away from your longest streak ever."
"Your bench has gone up 12.5kg in 6 weeks. That's elite progress."
"You haven't missed a Monday in 5 weeks. Consistency is your superpower."
"Last week you crushed legs. This week, let's match that energy on push."
"It's deload week. Go lighter. Trust the process."
The Streak as Identity
🔥
The streak isn't a number in a corner — it's the biggest thing on screen. Giant 72px number. A bioluminescent glow that pulses with the heartbeat. A mini-heatmap showing the last 7 days. It's the first thing you see. It's your scoreboard. Breaking it would feel like losing something physical.
The Nudge Card
💡
A contextual insight below the hero. References your last session's performance and connects it to today's plan. "Last time you hit X, today you're going for Y." Makes you feel like the app remembers everything and is coaching you forward. Uses your actual data from set_logs.
The Living Aesthetic
💓
Every element breathes on a shared 2.4s heartbeat. The alive-dot, streak glow, readiness pulse, CTA aura — all synced. During workouts the rhythm accelerates. After completion it slows to resting. The app has a pulse like you do.
🧬
Membrane blobs drift behind the surface at 3.5% opacity. Muscle fiber lines contract inside exercise icons. Card borders have a rotating conic accent. Nothing is dead. But nothing is distracting — it's all peripheral, felt more than seen.
Layout Choices
🎨
Asymmetric bento grid for stats. The streak cell is tall and dominant (it's your identity). Momentum and volume are smaller supporting cells. No two cells are the same size. Creates visual hierarchy that says "this matters most."
📃
Exercise cards are still a list — but with living borders, fiber icons, set-pip progress, and weight on the right. The list is the right structure here because exercises are sequential. But each card feels alive, not dead.
What No Other App Does
❌
Strava: data-heavy but impersonal. Opens to a feed of other people.
Nike: beautiful but generic. Same screen for everyone.
Hevy/Strong: functional but dead. Spreadsheet with a skin.
Peloton: instructor-led, not self-led.
✔️
Repped opens with YOUR story. Your streak. Your milestone. Your data turned into a personal message. In a UI that breathes. No other app does all of this together.
