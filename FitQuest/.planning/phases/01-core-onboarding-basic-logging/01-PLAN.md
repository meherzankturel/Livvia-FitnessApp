## Success Criteria (Phase 1)
1. User completes onboarding, providing gender, weight, body composition, and device access, and sees a calculated success probability.
2. User selects a Physique Path and the choice persists in the UI.
3. Workout options (Express, Standard, Limit Break) are generated and selectable.
4. HUD appears during workouts with high‑contrast styling.
5. Voice commands correctly log reps; failure counter works via button/voice.
6. iWatch records pace and Time‑Under‑Tension, and provides a haptic cue before each set.
